Why Yenova

A club built the way we teach the subject — hands first.

The name pairs a brain with a circuit board on purpose. Yenova exists to close the gap between what's taught in a CS classroom and what's actually asked of you in the field, one running event at a time.

01

Skills over syllabus

Value-added courses like our web development bootcamp exist to teach what the curriculum has room to only mention.

02

Trained by practitioners

RECON Z brought real digital-forensics methodology into a two-day program. Job Essentials brought a founding engineer to talk hiring, honestly.

03

A department that shows up

Every program is run in the open by faculty and student coordinators together — same names organizing the workshop and the esports night.

04

Play is part of the culture

Mini Militia Esports isn't a detour from tech culture — it's how a department builds a community that sticks around for the harder stuff too.

05

Career-ready, not just exam-ready

Sessions like Job Essentials focus on the part nobody grades: resumes, interviews, and what a first employer is actually screening for.

06

Backed by an A+ NAAC institution

Every program runs under Yenepoya (Deemed to be University) — the accreditation is the department's, the initiative is Yenova's.
