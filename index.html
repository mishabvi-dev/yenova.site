About Us

Founded in 2023 at Yenepoya.

Yenova is an active student information technology club serving as a central hub for student collaboration, tech events, and practical skill-building outside of regular academic lectures at YIASCM in Mangaluru.

01

Skill Development

Organizing dedicated training sessions, coding projects, and workshops covering programming languages, tech tools, and emerging software.

02

Emerging Technologies

Helping members dive into fields like Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Cybersecurity.

03

Industry Connection

Hosting regular tech talks and interactive guest lectures with industry experts to bridge the gap between classroom theory and real careers.

04

Community Building

Creating an open network for tech enthusiasts and computer application students to share knowledge and build peer-to-peer technical networks.
